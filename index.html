Time log · iPhone · On the App Store

every15min

  1. A beat every 15 minutes

    The app and its home-screen widget count down to the next quarter hour. When it hits, jot a line. The rhythm can be anything from 5 to 60 minutes.

  2. Type one line and it's logged

    Like typing into a terminal: one sentence, an optional tag, and the timestamp is added for you.

  3. See where the day went

    A 24-hour bar marks the hours you logged, and tag counts show where your time actually goes.

  4. Find anything with find

    Type find #work and every matching entry is pulled up.

    Epilepsy meds & seizure diary · iPhone

    Goldie

    1. Meds that feel like a check-in

      Daily doses are grouped automatically. One tap marks them taken, and your streak grows day by day.

    2. Log a seizure in a few taps

      Step through when it happened, how severe, how long, possible triggers and consciousness, so you can explain it clearly at your next appointment.

    3. Count the safe days, not the seizures

      The home screen shows how many days it's been since your last seizure, so every calm day is visible.

    4. Help the people around you help you

      The emergency page copies your seizure guidance in one tap and calls emergency services or your contacts in one more.

    Meet Goldie

    Goldie is a logging tool and does not diagnose or give treatment advice. Numbers in the animation are examples.
