Time log · iPhone · On the App Store

every15min

  1. A beat every 15 minutes

    The app and its home-screen widget count down to the next quarter hour. When it hits, jot a line. The rhythm can be anything from 5 to 60 minutes.

  2. Type one line and it's logged

    Like typing into a terminal: one sentence, an optional tag, and the timestamp is added for you.

  3. See where the day went

    A 24-hour bar marks the hours you logged, and tag counts show where your time actually goes.

  4. Find anything with find

    Type find #work and every matching entry is pulled up.

    Epilepsy meds & seizure diary · iPhone

    Goldie

    1. Meds that feel like a check-in

      Daily doses are grouped automatically. One tap marks them taken, and your streak grows day by day.

    2. Log a seizure in a few taps

      Step through when it happened, how severe, how long, possible triggers and consciousness, so you can explain it clearly at your next appointment.

    3. Count the safe days, not the seizures

      The home screen shows how many days it's been since your last seizure, so every calm day is visible.

    4. Help the people around you help you

      The emergency page copies your seizure guidance in one tap and calls emergency services or your contacts in one more.

      Voice typing · iPhone and Mac

      Speechless

      1. Hold, talk, let go

        Hold the pad in the text field you’re in, say it, let go. The text goes in where the cursor is, without leaving the app.

      2. Talk the way you talk

        Chinese and English in one sentence is fine. Filler words and false starts come out, punctuation goes in. It doesn’t always get it right.

      3. Slide up to send or cancel

        While holding, slide up: left cancels, right sends when you let go, where the field has a Send key. ABC switches to ordinary typing.

      4. It works with the AI service you bring

        Add a service with your own API key. What you say goes only to services you added and agreed to. What you type isn’t sent.

      About

      No ad SDKs, no tracking, no sign‑up with me, and I can't see anything you keep in the apps. In every15min, Goldie and Code Buddy your records stay on your device. Speechless is the exception: after you agree, it sends what you say to the AI services you add. See its privacy policy.

      Do I need an account?
      Not with me. every15min, Goldie and Code Buddy need no account at all. Speechless needs an API key from the AI service you choose, and that means an account there.
      Does my data stay on my device?
      With every15min, Goldie and Code Buddy, yes. Speechless is the exception: what you say goes to the AI services you added, after you agree in the app. Its privacy policy says what goes where.
