Speechless: Voice Keyboard for iPhone

Hold. Talk. Let go.

A voice keyboard that works with the AI service you bring. Hold the pad, say it, let go, and the text goes in where the cursor is, without leaving the app you’re in.

In preparation for the App Store

Free app · iOS 17 or later · you add your own AI service and API key · light or dark, following your iPhone

Example

Can we push the design review to Thursday afternoon?

ABC,spaceSend

Hold, tap or slide.

The whole pad is one key. It lights up with your voice, so you can see it’s listening.

  • Hold and talk

    Let go and the text goes in where the cursor is.

  • Tap to start, tap to stop

    For when you don’t want to hold. It stops by itself after a minute without speech.

  • Slide up while holding

    Left cancels. Right sends when you let go, in fields whose return key is Send, Search or Go.

  • Undo, or say it again

    Both show up after a sentence goes in. Undo takes it back out; Say again takes it out and starts listening.

Say it the way you’d say it.

Chinese and English in one sentence is fine. Filler words and false starts come out, punctuation goes in.

You say
um so I think we should ship it on Monday, no, ship it on Tuesday
It types
I think we should ship it on Tuesday.
You say
嗯帮我 review 一下那个那个首页的 PR 呃 merge 之前跑一下 test
It types
帮我 review 一下首页的 PR，merge 之前跑一下 test。

These are examples; results depend on the models you pick. With OpenRouter, two speech models listen to the same recording by default, so a word one of them misheard can be caught; with other services that check is optional. It does not always get it right, so read it before you send it.

What it stores and what Accessibility is for: privacy policy.

Where your voice goes.

Straight to the services you chose. Before the first use of each one, the app names it and its address and asks you. If you don’t agree, nothing goes there, and you can still type.

Your iPhone

Sends what you dictate with your own API keys. There is no server of mine in between.

The service you chose for recognition

Gets the recording when you finish, or at a pause in the middle so you wait less, and the words in your dictionary as hints.

The service you chose for cleanup

Gets the transcript, and the words in your dictionary as hints.

With OpenRouter

The default models are ElevenLabs Scribe v2 and AssemblyAI Universal‑3.6 Pro for recognition, with Mistral AI’s Voxtral Small Transcribe 3 as a backup when the first is slow, and DeepSeek V4.1 Flash or GLM‑5.3 Flash for cleanup, run by a host OpenRouter chooses among those that qualify.

Whichever models you pick, every request asks for zero data retention and no data collection. The few exceptions are in the privacy policy. OpenRouter (USA) keeps metadata, such as time, model and token counts, for billing.

With any other service

What you dictate goes straight from your iPhone to that service, under its own terms and privacy policy. Speechless can’t set zero retention there: how long the service keeps your data and what it does with it is between you and the service. It may be processed in another country, for example in China. How the services handle your data: see the links in the privacy policy.

Stored only on your iPhone

  • Your API keys, one per service, in the iOS Keychain; each is sent only to its own service
  • Settings, and the dictionary itself
  • The last 30 dictations, as text
  • Audio of sentences not transcribed yet
  • A technical log without what you said, 14 days

Never reaches me

I receive no audio, text, keys or usage data. The app has no analytics, no ads, no tracking and no third‑party SDKs. What you type with the ABC keyboard is not stored, logged or sent.

Help

Setup, how to use it, and answers to common questions.

Questions or problems? Email tonybingpoon@gmail.com. I read and answer every message myself.

Requirements

  • An iPhone with iOS 17 or later, or a Mac with macOS 14 or later. The interface is in English and Simplified Chinese.
  • For voice: an internet connection, an AI service you add with your own API key, Allow Full Access for the keyboard, microphone permission, and your consent in the app to send what you say to that service.
  • For typing with the ABC keyboard: nothing else. It works offline, without Full Access and without a key.
  • Availability: Speechless is in preparation for the App Store. At first it won’t be offered in China mainland or the EU.

Setting up

  1. Open the Speechless app and allow the microphone.
  2. In Settings › General › Keyboard › Keyboards › Add New Keyboard, choose Speechless. Then tap Speechless in that list and turn on Allow Full Access.
  3. In the app, under AI services, add a service and paste its API key. The app names the service and its address and asks whether you agree to send what you say there. If you don’t, you can still type.
  4. After you agree, the app loads the service’s list of models and tries recognition (one second of silence) and cleanup (one short sample sentence) once with your key, so you know both work. These two small test requests contain nothing you said. Then pick one model for recognition and one for cleanup; with OpenRouter, recommended models are already set.
  5. Tap a text field, touch and hold the globe key, and choose Speechless.

Choosing a service

  • You need an account with the service and an API key from it. OpenRouter’s own terms require account holders to be adults; other services set their own rules.
  • OpenRouter is the default. With it, the app lists only models that OpenRouter marks as zero‑retention.
  • You can also add OpenAI, Alibaba Cloud Model Studio (Qwen; Beijing or Singapore region), Volcengine Ark (Doubao; recognition is done by a general model that can listen, not a dedicated speech model), SiliconFlow (China or international), or any other service with OpenAI‑compatible endpoints by its https address.
  • Recognition and cleanup can use different services.
  • Availability in your country, price, quality in your language, speed and data terms differ from service to service. Read the service’s own terms and privacy policy before you add it.
  • Zero data retention is requested only with OpenRouter. With any other service, that service’s terms apply.

Using it

  • Hold the pad, talk, and let go. Or tap to start and tap again to stop.
  • Slide up while holding: left cancels; right sends when you let go, if the field’s return key is Send, Search or Go.
  • After a sentence goes in, Undo takes it back out, and Say again lets you say it over.
  • Touch and hold the punctuation key for more marks. Hold space and slide to move the cursor. Hold delete to keep deleting.
  • One recording can be up to 3 minutes long. When you started it with a tap, it stops by itself after a minute without speech.
  • Put names and product names in the dictionary in the app. They are sent along as hints when you dictate.

Common questions

Do I need an account?

Not with me. For voice you need an API key from the AI service you choose, which means an account there. Typing needs neither.

Voice doesn’t work

Check that the microphone is allowed, Allow Full Access is on, you’re online, and in the app a service with a working key is added, you agreed to it, and models are picked. If all of that is fine, see whether your account with the service has credit left.

Why does it ask for Allow Full Access?

iOS gives a keyboard no network and no microphone without it, so voice can’t work. The ABC keyboard doesn’t need it. With Full Access on, Speechless still doesn’t store, log or send what you type.

Does it listen in the background?

No. It records only while you dictate, with the system’s microphone indicator and the keyboard’s own animation showing. Recording stops after 3 minutes at most, and after a minute without speech when you started it with a tap.

What does it cost?

The app is free, with no in-app purchase and no ads. The AI services you add bill you directly for what you use. I sell no AI usage and get nothing from any of them.

It got a word wrong

That happens. Tap Undo or Say again, or fix it by hand. If it’s a name it keeps missing, add it to the dictionary in the app.

The key says Return instead of Send

In some apps, Send is the app’s own button, and a keyboard can’t press it. There the key says Return; tap the app’s button instead.

My music stops

Music pauses while it records.

It’s slow

How long you wait depends on your network, on the service and on the models you picked.

Speechless isn’t there in a password field

iOS replaces third-party keyboards in password fields. Some apps also don’t allow them.

A sentence didn’t come out

Its audio stays on your iPhone until it has been transcribed, so you can try it again. Your last 30 dictations are in the app as text.

How do I stop it sending anything?

In the Speechless app, go to AI services, choose the service and tap Withdraw consent. Nothing more is sent there. You can do this for each service at any time. Typing still works.

How do I delete my data?

In the app, Clear deletes your recent dictations. Erase all data, under Privacy, deletes the keys, dictionary, history, audio and log. Removing a service under AI services removes its key too.

Uninstalling

In the app, under Privacy, tap Erase all data first, then delete the app. Deleting the app removes what it stored, but iOS can keep Keychain items after an app is deleted, and that is where the API keys are. You can also delete a key in your account with the service.

Privacy

Speechless sends what you dictate to the AI services you added, after you agree. What you type is not sent. See the privacy policy.
